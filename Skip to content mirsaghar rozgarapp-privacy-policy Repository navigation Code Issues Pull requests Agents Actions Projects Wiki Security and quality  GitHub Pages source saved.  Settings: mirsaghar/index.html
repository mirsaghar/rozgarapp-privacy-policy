RozgarApp Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RozgarApp collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the RozgarApp mobile application.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, RozgarApp may collect information such as your email address, account information, task activity, and in-app points.

We may also collect technical information such as device information, app activity, and diagnostic information when necessary to operate and improve the application.

How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide and operate app features.
  • Track tasks and in-app points.
  • Maintain application security.
  • Improve application performance and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests.

Firebase

RozgarApp uses Google Firebase services to provide certain application functions, including authentication and data storage. Information processed by Firebase is handled according to Google's applicable privacy practices.

Advertising

RozgarApp uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, advertising data, and usage information to provide and measure advertisements, subject to Google's policies.

In-App Points

RozgarApp may provide non-transferable in-app points through supported activities. These points have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money or transferred to another person.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain security, comply with applicable requirements, and resolve disputes.

Children's Privacy

RozgarApp is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services such as Google Firebase and Google AdMob. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or RozgarApp, please contact us through the support contact provided on the application's Google Play listing.
